Set up Arr

Arr finds and downloads movies and TV shows for your Plex server. This takes a few minutes, and nothing is downloaded yet.

1 Sign in to Plex

Arr uses your Plex account to find your server and libraries. Your password is entered on plex.tv, never here.

4 Downloads

Downloads finish here, then move into your library. Keep it on the same drive as your library to save space.

Turn this off to try Arr out first: it will search and plan, but not download.

Recommended. Arr checks once a day for a new version and installs it, and goes back to the old one if the new one doesn't start.

Optional: a TMDB key, to find movies you don't have yet

TV shows work without it. Get a free key at themoviedb.org/settings/api (choose “Developer”, any app name is fine), then paste the API key or the read access token.
