Set up Arr

Arr finds and downloads movies and TV shows for your Plex server. This takes a few minutes, and nothing is downloaded yet.

1 Sign in to Plex

Arr uses your Plex account to find your server and libraries. Your password is entered on plex.tv, never here.

Signed in to Plex.

If something goes wrong during setup or later, Arr sends a short note about the error to its developer (through Sentry, stored in the EU). Names, folders, addresses and passwords are removed first, and nothing about your library or what you watch is sent. What's sent.

2 Plex on this computer

Plex isn't on this computer yet. Plex is the free app that plays your movies and shows on your TV, phone and browser, and Arr can install it for you.

Your computer will ask for your password, as it does when you install any app.

Optional: a TMDB key, to find movies you don't have yet

TV shows work without it. Get a free key at themoviedb.org/settings/api (choose “Developer”, any app name is fine), then paste the API key or the read access token.
